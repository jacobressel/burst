Quick rules

Competitive BURST: 2–10 players. Play the selected 3–6 rounds. Score points from Matches and use Powers at any time. Discards reshuffle into the draw pile as needed. If Finale is ON, bank Powers through all earlier rounds; the final round is Powers only. Highest score wins.

Swap: move a card orthogonally to create a Match 3+. With Slide ON, the chosen card may move 1 or 2 spaces; a 2-space move shifts the displaced cards backward along its path. Choose Tap Swap or Slide-to-Match input when starting the game.

Glitter: choose one visible firework type. Global flips every card showing it. For Adjacent, choose Glitter Action and tap any firework card to flip it and matching cards in its 8 surrounding spaces. With Tap to Glitter ON, a quick tap on a card performs Glitter immediately while sliding still performs a Swap.

Board size: choose 3–8 columns (X) and 3–8 rows (Y). Matching, gravity, powers, and bot search use the selected dimensions.

Bot difficulty: Easy evaluates only the current move and randomly chooses among its top 3 moves. Medium looks one deterministic visible Match wave ahead and randomly chooses among its top 3 moves. Hard evaluates the current Match plus two subsequent cascade Match waves and chooses the strongest expected move, using only public odds for unknown refills or hidden flips. Bots never inspect hidden reverse faces, future draw cards, or deck order.

Firework types: each firework card face and its Power can be enabled independently in setup. Card OFF removes that type from the deck and disables its Power; Power OFF keeps the card matching/scoring normally but Match 4+ does not award that Power.

Dud Firework (optional): Dud is an optional non-scoring card face set from 1% to 20% of all card faces. It moves normally and can be Glittered, cleared, or flipped by powers. Flipping reveals its normal opposite face, which can match. Dud has no power and is not required for the Finale. If Dud self-match flip is ON, any 3+ Dud formation recognized by the same merged match geometry as Fireworks flips itself in place. Dud formations score 0 and never earn Powers.

Match 3: +1 point, clear, cascade, refill.

Merged Match: same-firework matching lines that overlap or touch orthogonally score as one match. Count unique cards to determine 4+ power eligibility. A T made of two 3-card lines is one 5-card match: 1 point and a power. Diagonal-only contact and separate matches remain separate. A lone 3-card match remains Match 3.

Match 4+: +1 point, clear, and create/earn its firework power.

Powers: Comet, Ring, Crossette, and Peony can each be set to Clear or Flip their normal target pattern. Palm is always an Extra Swap and does nothing beyond the Match created by that Swap. Comet normally targets a column; with the Row/Column option on, the player chooses either direction each time Comet is fired.

BURST: Grand Finale: 1–10 players. Work alone or together. The draw pile is finite and never reshuffled. Earn each active Power exactly once into the shared bank. Once all are earned, normal actions stop; fire every Power before the draw pile runs out.

SHORT FUSE: 2–10 players. Play the selected 3–6 rounds with a finite draw pile. Score Match Points normally. If your turn needs a refill card and none remain, BUST! The round ends immediately. The Busted player takes last place and 1 Finish Point; everyone else ranks by Match Points, with 1st earning N Finish Points, 2nd N−1, and so on. The Busted player starts the next round. After the selected number of rounds, most Finish Points wins.

Power limit: when One Power per round is ON, each player may fire one Power in each regular round. In the final round the limit is removed, including for Powers earned during that round.

Earn cap: when Max one of each Power earned per round is ON, each player can earn each active Power type only once during that round, even if the Power is spent before another 4+ match of that type.

Unlimited earning before final round: when ON, every enabled Match 4+ in non-final rounds earns its Power and duplicates may stack. In the final round, Match 4+ still scores but earns no new Power. This setting overrides the duplicate-bank and per-type earn limits while active.

Next-round Powers: when Earned Powers usable next round is ON, newly earned Powers are queued and become usable when the next round begins. They cannot be fired in the round they are earned.

Glitter / Peony targeting: choose Both Global, Glitter Adjacent / Peony Global, or Glitter Global / Peony Adjacent. Adjacent targeting affects the tapped center card and matching cards in the 8 surrounding spaces.

Crossette: affects the center card and four orthogonally adjacent cards (+) by default. The alternate X pattern may be selected in setup.

Cards cleared directly by a power score 0. Matches caused afterward score normally.
