Quick rules

Competitive BURST: 2–10 players. Play the selected 3–6 rounds. Score points from Matches and use Powers at any time. Discards reshuffle into the draw pile as needed. If Finale is ON, bank Powers through all earlier rounds; the final round is Powers only. Highest score wins.

Swap: swap two orthogonally adjacent cards to create a Match 3+. Choose Tap Swap or Slide Swap when starting the game.

Glitter: choose one visible firework type. Global flips every card showing it. For Adjacent, choose Glitter Action and tap any firework card to flip it and matching cards in its 8 surrounding spaces.

Match 3: +1 point, clear, cascade, refill.

Merged Match (optional): same-firework matching lines that overlap or touch orthogonally score as one match. Count unique cards to determine 4+ power eligibility. A T made of two 3-card lines is one 5-card match: 1 point and a power. Diagonal-only contact and separate matches remain separate. A lone 3-card match remains Match 3.

Match 4+: +1 point, clear, create/earn its power, and optionally trigger the same-color bonus Glitter depending on the selected playtest setting.

Powers: Comet, Ring, Crossette, and Peony can each be set to Clear or Flip their normal target pattern. Palm is always an Extra Swap and does nothing beyond the Match created by that Swap. Comet normally targets a column; with the Row/Column option on, the player chooses either direction each time Comet is fired.

BURST: Grand Finale: 1–10 players. Work alone or together. The draw pile is finite and never reshuffled. Earn each active Power exactly once into the shared bank. Once all are earned, normal actions stop; fire every Power before the draw pile runs out.

SHORT FUSE: 2–10 players. Play the selected 3–6 rounds with a finite draw pile. Score Match Points normally. If your turn needs a refill card and none remain, BUST! The round ends immediately. The Busted player takes last place and 1 Finish Point; everyone else ranks by Match Points, with 1st earning N Finish Points, 2nd N−1, and so on. The Busted player starts the next round. After the selected number of rounds, most Finish Points wins.

Power limit: when One Power per turn is ON, each player may fire one Power per turn until the final round. In the final round the limit is removed, including for Powers earned during that round.

Glitter / Peony targeting: choose Both Global, Glitter Adjacent / Peony Global, or Glitter Global / Peony Adjacent. Adjacent targeting affects the tapped center card and matching cards in the 8 surrounding spaces.

Crossette: affects the center and four diagonal neighbors (X) by default. Turn on Orthogonal (+) pattern to affect the center and four orthogonal neighbors instead.

Cards cleared directly by a power score 0. Matches caused afterward score normally.
