    Quick rules

    Controls: Swipe to Match moves the selected card directly with your finger; release at least halfway toward a neighbor to Swap, or earlier to return it. Tap to Glitter, when enabled, responds only to a quick unmoved tap. Both controls can be chosen in Gameplay Options.

    Competitive: Highest score after the selected rounds wins. Swap an orthogonally adjacent card to make a Match 3+, or Glitter a selected card and matching cards in its eight neighboring spaces. A Match 3+ scores one point; Match 4+ also earns its firework Power. Duplicate Powers stack.

    Powers: One Power may be used per regular round. Crossette clears the center and its four orthogonal neighbors (+). Ring flips the eight cards around its center. Peony flips every visible card of the selected type. Comet clears a chosen row or column.

    Finale: Powers earned before the final round are banked. During the final round, Swap and Glitter remain available, all banked Powers may be used, and Match 4+ scores and cascades normally without earning another Power.

    BURST Infinite: Play alone without rounds or a natural end. Complete randomized goals, use Powers freely, and forfeit any goal to get a new one. T and L goals count in all four quarter-turn orientations. Session score, matches, and goal history continue until you start a new game.

    Short Fuse: The finite draw supply makes refills risky; when a refill cannot be drawn, that player busts and the round ends.

    Grand Finale Challenge: Collect all four Powers in the shared bank, then fire them before the finite draw supply runs out.

    Duds: 20% of the deck’s card faces are Duds. They never match, score, or earn Powers. Glitter and applicable Powers can flip them; clearing Powers can remove them.

    How to Play — Text Instructions

    Swap neighboring cards to make a line of three or more matching fireworks. You have two actions each turn: Swap or Glitter, in any combination.

    Glitter flips the chosen card and matching cards touching it, including diagonally. Duds never match, but Glitter and applicable Powers can flip them.

    Each distinct match earns 1 point, regardless of its size or shape. Connected same-type matching lines merge into one match. Replacement cards can cascade into further matches, each worth another point.

    A match with 4 or more cards also earns that firework’s Power. In Competitive, bank Powers until the final round. Then use all banked Powers alongside your two normal actions. No new Powers are earned in that round.

    Comet: clear a chosen row or column. Crossette: clear the center and its four orthogonal neighbors (+). Ring: flip the eight surrounding cards; the center stays. Peony: flip every card of a chosen face type across the board. Select a Power in the bank first, then tap any grid card to target it. The target need not match the Power, and its firework type does not have to appear on the grid. Direct Power clears do not score; matches created afterward do.

    In Competitive, the highest score after the chosen number of rounds wins. Expand Game Mode instructions above for the other modes.
